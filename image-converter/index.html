Why Convert Images to WebP Format?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the web. WebP images are significantly smaller than PNGs and JPGs (often 25% to 35% smaller) while maintaining equal visual quality, making your websites load drastically faster.
